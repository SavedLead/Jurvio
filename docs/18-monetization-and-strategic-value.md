# Jurvio Gate 7.5: Monetization and Strategic Value

**Status:** Decision draft  
**Date:** 2026-07-19  
**Depends on:** Approved Gates 1–7

## 1. Product principle

Core public legal information remains free. Jurvio charges for monitoring, speed, organization, workflow, advanced analysis, reports, collaboration and governed data access.

The company must be built as a strong standalone business while deliberately accumulating assets that major legal, financial-data and AI companies cannot reproduce quickly or cheaply.

## 2. Initial revenue architecture

| Offering | Directional price | Customer | Core value |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Public and discovery users | Readable cases, explanations, basic search and limited follows |
| Jurvio Plus | $29/month | Individuals, journalists and investigators | 25 watches, daily change explanations, saved searches and limited reports |
| Jurvio Professional | $99/month | Attorneys and business professionals | 250 watches, near-real-time alerts, entity monitoring, exports and higher research limits |
| Jurvio Team | $299/month | Firms and investigative teams | Five seats, shared workspaces, larger limits and administration |
| Litigation Report | $19–$49 once | SEO visitors researching a person/company | Cited litigation history, identity caveats, timelines and dispositions |
| API/Enterprise | Later/contracted | Legal technology and data customers | Governed feeds, webhooks, bulk access and service commitments |

Prices and limits are configuration, not hard-coded policy. Test willingness to pay before expanding scope.

## 3. Free-to-paid boundary

Always free:

- readable case, opinion, party, attorney, judge, court and concept pages;
- sourced procedural explanations and citation-context previews;
- core documents where rights permit;
- basic search;
- limited follows and weekly digest.

Paid:

- increased watch capacity;
- immediate or high-frequency alerts;
- saved and advanced searches;
- cross-entity monitoring;
- “what changed” intelligence;
- reports and exports;
- team workflow;
- governed API/webhooks;
- bounded AI research usage.

No payment can alter case facts, editorial treatment, rankings or legal explanations.

## 4. First monetizable product

“Monitor any case, person, company, attorney, law firm, judge, policy or legal topic and receive a readable, sourced explanation whenever something important changes.”

Every alert must state what happened, why it matters, what changed, what remains unresolved and what may happen next. Raw docket-entry notifications alone are not sufficient differentiation.

## 5. One-time litigation reports

Reports may include matched public litigation, identity confidence, party role, case posture, timeline, disposition and source links.

Requirements:

- no unsupported identity merging;
- conspicuous source and confidence disclosures;
- not a consumer report;
- prohibited use notice for employment, credit, housing, insurance and other regulated eligibility decisions;
- correction and dispute path;
- counsel review before commercialization.

## 6. Attorney and law-firm products

Factual profiles remain free. Future paid products may include verified profile ownership, submitted biographies, publication feeds, analytics, alerts and clearly labeled promotion.

- Paid placement is disclosed.
- Paid links receive appropriate sponsored attributes.
- Payment cannot change litigation facts.
- Third-party ratings are not copied without rights.
- Submitted analysis is labeled by source and separated from Jurvio explanation.

## 7. Strategic crown jewels

1. Provenance-backed litigation knowledge graph.
2. Case Understanding and Legal Procedure Intelligence systems.
3. Entity resolution and identity-confidence corpus.
4. Litigation Change Intelligence and alert graph.
5. Citation Context and proposition-level treatment graph.
6. Public SEO/AI discovery distribution.
7. Privacy-safe Legal Demand Graph.
8. Proprietary legal evaluation and correction dataset.
9. Professional workflow adoption and recurring revenue.
10. Portable, well-documented data and technology rights.

Generic LLM summaries and publicly obtainable PDFs are not durable acquisition assets by themselves.

## 8. Strategic-acquirer lens

Potential strategic categories include legal publishers, practice-management platforms, financial/business intelligence, AI platforms and legal-technology consolidators.

Every major roadmap item is evaluated on:

1. public adoption;
2. recurring or transactional revenue;
3. proprietary data/technology accumulation;
4. strategic-acquirer value;
5. cost and operational burden;
6. trust, rights and legal risk.

## 9. Metrics

- organic and AI-referred traffic;
- meaningful indexed pages and party/company rankings;
- professional active users;
- follows, saved searches and alert engagement;
- free-to-paid conversion;
- MRR/ARR, retention, expansion and revenue concentration;
- resolved entities and supported relationships;
- corpus coverage, freshness and correction rate;
- summary/citation accuracy;
- API demand and usage;
- acquisition, OCR, AI and infrastructure cost per engaged entity.

## 10. Revenue-linked spending

- Initial hosting ceiling: $250/month.
- Initial combined AI/OCR ceiling: $500/month.
- Paid document acquisition is demand-driven.
- Increase recurring infrastructure only for measured capacity, reliability or revenue needs.
- Target infrastructure below 20% of established recurring revenue, excluding unusual licensed-data campaigns.
- Require approval for any vendor capable of producing unbounded usage charges.

## 11. Milestones

1. First paid customer.
2. $1,000 MRR with evidence of repeated use.
3. $5,000 MRR with cohort retention measurement.
4. $20,000 MRR with team/API demand validation.
5. Repeatable acquisition channel and positive contribution margin.
6. Strategic partnerships or inbound interest without compromising independence.

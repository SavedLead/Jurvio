# ADR 0005: Monetization and Understanding Moat

**Status:** Proposed  
**Date:** 2026-07-19

## Context

Jurvio must grow through free public discovery while creating early revenue, differentiated product value and strategic assets that major incumbents cannot cheaply reproduce.

## Decision

Subject to Gate 7.5 and Gate 8 approval:

- Keep core legal information and explanation free.
- Monetize monitoring, readable change intelligence, advanced research, reports, collaboration and governed APIs.
- Test $29 individual, $99 professional and $299 team subscriptions.
- Build the Understanding, Legal Procedure Intelligence and Citation Context systems as connected, provenance-backed data products.
- Build Cases in the News, Government/Public Law and Supreme Court experiences on the same neutral explanation foundation.
- Accumulate a defensible knowledge graph, demand graph, evaluation corpus and professional workflow adoption.
- Enforce evidence-status, neutrality, rights and prediction safeguards.
- Tie infrastructure and enrichment spending increases to measured demand and revenue.

## Consequences

Jurvio gains a coherent acquisition funnel and differentiated intelligence layer but assumes substantial taxonomy, evaluation, editorial-quality and correction responsibilities. The platform must resist generic AI output, partisan framing, unsupported identity linkage and feature expansion without evidence.

## Reconsideration triggers

- Users will not pay for monitoring or change intelligence.
- One-time reports create unacceptable legal/compliance risk.
- Annotation density harms reading.
- Accuracy cannot satisfy both public comprehension and professional review.
- News/policy coverage creates editorial burden disproportionate to adoption.
- A different workflow demonstrates materially stronger retention or strategic value.

## Approval

This ADR becomes Accepted only after explicit approval of Gate 7.5 and Gate 8A.
